# Product image usage

The six included files in `static/photos/` are **AI-generated generic product illustrations** for your university demonstration, not photographs of a specified item or brand. They may be shown as illustrative samples in the application.

The older `download_product_photos.py` tool is optional and can replace them with third-party Wikimedia images. If you do that, review each source's license and attribution separately. Nothing needs to be downloaded for this deployment package.
